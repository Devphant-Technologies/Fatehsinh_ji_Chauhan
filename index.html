Post-Launch Support

Bug Fixes Free for Life. AMC for Everything Else.

Any bug or technical error on the site is fixed free of charge for the lifetime of the website — no time limit, no expiry. The AMC below is a separate monthly plan for everything beyond bug fixing: cloud hosting, content updates, and ongoing support. It's entirely the Office's call whether and when to take it.

How the AMC Works

The website runs on AWS cloud infrastructure — this needs an active hosting bill and monitoring to stay online. Beyond bug fixes (which are always free), the Office may also want to update photos, or add new Recognition and Community & Events entries over time. The ₹12,000/month AMC is a single, simple plan that covers all of this — cloud hosting, content updates, and general support — in one predictable monthly fee.

Without the AMC, the AWS cloud bill and any technical maintenance become the client's own responsibility to arrange directly. With the AMC, Devphant × Mood Board handles all of it under one predictable monthly fee.
